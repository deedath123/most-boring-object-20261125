Entry for the make.fun bounty: [The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3)

One line, at most 200 characters: a funny, true review of a boring
everyday object. This entry reviews a doorstop — a one-star review of
214 days of thankless door-holding, capped by it watching its owner
trip over it and carrying on like nothing happened.

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
